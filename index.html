JobHound

A personal job-application tool.

JobHound is a single-user automation tool built and operated by Maniram Tatipamula. It monitors a private Telegram group for job leads, checks them against a personal set of eligibility rules, and — for matches that list an email address to apply to — composes and sends a tailored application email through the owner's own Gmail account.

It is not a public product, is not available for anyone else to sign up for or use, and does not collect or process data belonging to anyone other than its single owner/operator.

This page exists to satisfy Google OAuth's app-homepage requirement for the Google account permissions JobHound uses (sending email on the owner's behalf, and reading a small set of files from the owner's own Google Drive). See the privacy policy for details on exactly what access is used and why.
