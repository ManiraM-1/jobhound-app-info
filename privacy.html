Privacy Policy

JobHound is a single-user personal tool. There is exactly one user: its owner and operator, Maniram Tatipamula. It is not distributed, marketed, or made available to anyone else, and it does not knowingly collect, process, or store data belonging to any other person.

What it accesses, and why

  • Gmail — send only (gmail.send scope). JobHound can send email as the owner, to compose and submit job applications. It cannot read, search, or modify any existing mail — the scope it requests makes that technically impossible, not just a policy promise.
  • Google Drive — read only (drive.readonly scope). JobHound reads a small, fixed set of files the owner maintains there — resume PDFs and a personal "application kit" of facts/skills/projects — so it always applies with current, accurate information. It does not write, modify, or delete anything in Drive, and does not access files outside what it's configured to look for.
  • A private Telegram group, read-only, to find job leads. This is unrelated to the Google account access above.

What it does with that access

Extracted job details (company, role, pay, location) and the content of composed application emails are used only to send that one application. Full source text of any Telegram post is never stored persistently. No data collected here is sold, shared, or made available to any third party.

Data retention

A small local database tracks which job postings have already been acted on, to avoid duplicate applications. It contains structured job metadata only (company, role, outcome) — never full message text, never email credentials, and it is private to the owner.

Contact

Questions about this tool or its data handling: contact the owner at the email address associated with the Google account that authorizes it.
